Types of words

Content Words
  • moku: eat, drink, food
  • ike: bad, evil
  • toki: to talk, language, hello
  • mi: I, we, my, our...
  • jan: person
  • tawa: to move
Grammar particles
  • li: Introduces predicate
  • e: Introduces direct object
  • en: Separates sentence subjects
  • anu: or
  • la: Marks end of context
  • o: Calling someone, commands, or wishes.
